The Father of the Atomic Dawn

BY Christopher nolan

holder

Oppenheimer follows the brilliant physicist J. Robert Oppenheimer as he leads the secret Manhattan Project during World War II. Haunted by his own genius, he races to build the atomic bomb before the enemy does. But when his creation unleashes unimaginable power, he’s forced to confront the moral weight of what he’s done. The film explores ambition, responsibility, and the moment humanity changed forever.
