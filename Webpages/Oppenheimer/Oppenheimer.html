Plot

Oppenheimer tells the story of J. Robert Oppenheimer, the brilliant physicist who led the Manhattan Project. He wrestles with the moral weight of creating the atomic bomb. The film explores his personal conflicts, political pressures, and relationships. In the end, it portrays the triumph of science shadowed by the devastation it unleashed.
