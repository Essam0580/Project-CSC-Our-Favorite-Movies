History Behind Oppenheimer Movie

The Story of Creation

The movie "Oppenheimer" was crafted to tell the story of the brilliant physicist J. Robert Oppenheimer, who played a key role in the Manhattan Project during World War II. The production aimed to portray both the historical and moral complexities of creating the atomic bomb.
