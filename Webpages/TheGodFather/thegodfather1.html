The Godfather

A classic masterpiece directed by Francis Ford Coppola

Movie Summary

The Godfather (1972) follows the powerful Italian-American Corleone crime family, focusing on the rise of Michael Corleone as he transforms from a reluctant outsider to a ruthless mafia boss.

Main Characters

  • Vito Corleone – The Godfather
  • Michael Corleone – His youngest son
  • Sonny Corleone – Hot-headed eldest son
  • Tom Hagen – Consigliere
