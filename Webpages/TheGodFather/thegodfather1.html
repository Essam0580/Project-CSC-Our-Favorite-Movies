Movie Summary

Released in 1972, The Godfather is a crime drama that chronicles the Corleone mafia family, starring Marlon Brando and Al Pacino. It explores power, family obligations, and the cost of ambition.

Main Characters

Vito Corleone

Michael Corleone

Sonny Corleone
