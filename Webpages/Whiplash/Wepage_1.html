WHIPLASH

PLOT SUMMARY



Whiplash follows Andrew Neiman, a young and ambitious jazz drummer who dreams of becoming one of the greats. When he enrolls at a prestigious music conservatory, he comes under the mentorship of Terence Fletcher, a strict and intimidating instructor. Fletcher’s brutal methods push Andrew to his physical and emotional limits, forcing him to confront the cost of greatness and the fine line between dedication and obsession.

Through their intense relationship, Andrew struggles with ambition, perfectionism, and personal sacrifice, all while trying to prove himself as a top-tier musician.
